Concert Composition

Commission original concert works for ensembles, recitals, and special performance occasions. Tailored meticulously to your ensemble's instrumentation and artistic vision.

Arrangement & Orchestration

High-caliber arrangements and orchestrations of existing concert, film, or game music, re-imagined for various instrumentations from chamber duos to full orchestra.
